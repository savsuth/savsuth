I work across data, artificial intelligence, and software engineering, drawn to the decisions that shape what a system can become. My projects take me from the mathematics of differentiation to the coordination of concurrent services and the context behind AI decisions. Across that range, I keep returning to the same ambition: building software whose foundations can support what comes next.

I enjoy work that moves quickly and rewards a deeper understanding of what sits beneath the surface. Small architectural choices accumulate into the performance, reliability, and possibilities of an entire product. As AI changes how we build and what we can attempt, I want to help shape the infrastructure that carries those ideas forward, with the curiosity to explore new directions and the discipline to make them hold up.
